## 2. Services

**Q: What services does ACOB offer?**
A: ACOB offers solar panel installation, inverter systems, smart energy meters, backup power solutions, energy audits and consulting, mini-grid solutions, EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) services, design and installation of LED street lighting infrastructure, and Operations & Maintenance (O&M).

**Q: What is a mini-grid?**
A: A mini-grid is a small-scale electricity distribution network that can operate independently from the main grid. ACOB deploys hybrid solar mini-grids to power communities, farms, hospitals, and other users in underserved areas.

**Q: Does ACOB install solar panels?**
A: Yes. Solar panel installation is one of ACOB's core services, for residential, commercial, and community applications.

**Q: Does ACOB do energy audits?**
A: Yes. ACOB offers professional energy audit services to assess energy consumption and identify opportunities to reduce costs and improve efficiency. Visit /services/professional-energy-audit for more.

**Q: What is EPC?**
A: EPC stands for Engineering, Procurement, and Construction. ACOB provides end-to-end EPC services for energy projects, managing the entire process from design to installation. See /services/engineering-procurement-construction.

**Q: Does ACOB install streetlights?**
A: Yes. ACOB designs and installs high-density LED street lighting infrastructure for government and institutional clients. See /services/streetlighting-infrastructure-project-development.

**Q: What is a captive power solution?**
A: A captive power solution is a dedicated on-site power generation system for a business or facility, independent of the main utility grid. ACOB installs solar-based captive power systems for commercial and industrial clients. See /services/captive-power-solutions.

**Q: Does ACOB provide inverter systems?**
A: Yes. Inverter systems are part of ACOB's product and service offering for backup power and solar energy conversion.

**Q: Does ACOB do operations and maintenance?**
A: Yes. ACOB provides O&M (Operations and Maintenance) services to ensure long-term performance and reliability of installed systems.

**Q: Does ACOB provide smart energy meters?**
A: Yes. Smart energy meters are part of ACOB's solutions for monitoring and managing energy consumption.

**Q: Can ACOB help my business reduce its electricity costs?**
A: Yes. ACOB offers energy audits, captive power solutions, and solar installations designed to reduce your electricity costs. Visit our Services page at /services for more.

**Q: Does ACOB work with hospitals?**
A: Yes. ACOB has installed solar hybrid systems for hospitals and health facilities, particularly in underserved areas. See our Projects page at /projects for examples.

**Q: Does ACOB work with banks?**
A: Yes. ACOB has completed solar hybrid installations for banks and financial institutions.

**Q: Can ACOB power a whole community?**
A: Yes. ACOB specialises in mini-grid solutions that can power entire communities, farms, and productive enterprises. We have deployed over 15 hybrid solar mini-grids with 750kW peak capacity.
